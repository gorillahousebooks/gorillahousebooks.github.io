NanoQuest Map

Before the park fell, the terror came home.

Thirteen-year-old Mike Murray has a secret: his whole body is made of shape-shifting pixels. Convinced he's the lost son of visionary director Frank Nano, Mike rallies his friends to enter a filmmaking contest, using his powers to create special effects for their guerrilla superhero blockbuster.

But production stalls when Sal Destro, a vengeful rival from Frank's past, crashes the set. Armed with a horde of mechanical apes, Sal turns their suburban shoot into a war zone, hell-bent on extracting the living technology from Mike's DNA.

As the line between movies and reality blurs, Mike must master his glitching form to defeat Sal and finish his film. Will he survive long enough to claim his legacy? Or will his life end up on the cutting room floor?

Dream extreme, nanofans—and prepare yourself for the ultimate spin.

JACK McGUIGAN is also the writer of the Dog Walker novels and Agents of Paradox, a comic book. In his youth, he made movies under the pseudonym "John McGuigan" which can be found on the internet. He lives in Chicago with his family.
