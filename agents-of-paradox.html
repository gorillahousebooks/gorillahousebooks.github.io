Paradox

Jack McGuigan, John Fortune & Vanessa Kirby

"TIME BREAKS!"

Katie Baker's 1985 goes from bad to prehistoric when a rampaging T-Rex crashes her high school pep rally. She escapes through a wormhole with Samuel Dillon: a time-hardened soldier from the old west who's been patching up history since before she was born.

Together, they hunt a rogue cyborg warlord through the shattered streets of WWII Berlin and across the primordial Mongol plains. While Dillon struggles to protect a timeline that already cost him his wife and son, Katie must decide if she's fighting to save the future or running away from her past. As the clock winds down, they'll both learn that going back is never as easy as it seems.

What would you give up to rewrite history?

Collects Issues #1-5.

Written by JACK McGUIGAN
Art by JOHN FORTUNE
Colors by VANESSA KIRBY
