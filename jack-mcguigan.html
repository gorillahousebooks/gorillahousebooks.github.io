Jack McGuigan

Jack McGuigan is the author of the Dog Walker urban fantasy novels, the science fiction Frank Nano Saga (Nanoland, Nanoman), and Agents of Paradox, a time travel comic book. In his youth, he made movies under the pseudonym "John McGuigan" that can be found on the the internet. He lives in Chicago with his family.
